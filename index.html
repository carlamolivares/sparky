Meet Sparky

Sparky is a Unitree Go2 robot dog, and he's packed with tech! He maps the whole room with 360° LiDAR, keeps his balance with AI trained in simulation, and can be driven or programmed right from an app. In one walk across the floor, students get to see networking, programming, and AI working together up close.
